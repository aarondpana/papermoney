# Chart-guide source coverage

All 112 physical PDF pages were rendered and visually reviewed in ten labeled contact sheets before finalizing the bank. Original PDFs and private renders are excluded from the repository. The map records physical pages and extracted slide identifiers, including duplicated identifiers in the PDF; no arithmetic offset is used.

92 curated questions across 40 concepts: 80 require a chart (8 region-selection questions) and 12 use supplied hypothetical calculations. Each option has its own explanation. All illustrations are generated from original deterministic fictional bars. All prices, three-bar SMA settings, synthetic VWAP, sessions, numerical risk rules and decision conditions are explicitly educational interpretations, not copied historical data or attributed trading prescriptions. No future continuation is generated or revealed.

## Ambiguities and exclusions

- Small historical candle/indicator labels, exact scanner values, orders and execution details are not transcribed. Readable titles and major structures are used; uncertain numerical details are omitted.
- Page 62 mixes opening-range title and premarket diagram labels. Our questions supply an independent explicit opening-range definition rather than silently resolving it.
- Red-to-green on page 65 does not independently establish which platform price comparison is intended. Our examples explicitly stipulate prior close.
- Historical halt, squeeze, IPO, and news labels are treated as source labels, not independent event verification or current brokerage/regulatory guidance.
- The guide shows both successful-looking examples and traps. Candidate setup, a stipulated trigger and uncertain outcome remain distinct.
- Pattern names are hidden during unfiltered questions. Neutral SVG titles, accessible OHLC tables and A/B/C region labels contain observations only. The full source reference and pattern card are revealed with feedback. By Topic intentionally discloses the learner-selected topic.
- Supplemental pattern cards are sourced to the chart guide, never misattributed to the book.
